Lore · The tracklist

How RL Grime got here

His story, read like a DJ set: one year, one moment, one track to cue up.

2011

Clockwork

Henry Steinway, born in Los Angeles in 1991, records under the name Clockwork before reinventing himself.

2012

The trap turn

With the WeDidIt crew he becomes RL Grime. The Grapes EP and his first Halloween mix arrive the same year.

2014

VOID

His debut album. Huge, dark, cinematic trap, and the blueprint for everything after it.

▶ cue up: Core
2014

Tell Me

With What So Not. Still one of the defining festival records of the era.

▶ cue up: Tell Me
2017

Era and Reims

Two singles that went emotional and melodic without losing the weight.

▶ cue up: Reims
2018

NOVA

His second album, with Miguel, Julia Michaels, Chief Keef and Ty Dolla $ign.

▶ cue up: I Wanna Know (ft. Daya)
2019

Sable Valley opens

He launches his own label with "Arcus" featuring graves, then takes the roster on tour.

▶ cue up: Arcus
2020

Sable Valley Summer

The first label compilation, a showcase for trap's next generation. It became a yearly tradition.

▶ cue up: Sable Valley Summer Vol. 1
2023

PLAY

Album three, built like a game in three worlds: APEX, GRID and RUSH, with 070 Shake, Juelz and ISOxo.

▶ cue up: Pour Your Heart Out (ft. 070 Shake)
2024

Halloween XIII: The End

The Halloween mix series, started in 2012, closes with a thirteenth and final chapter.

▶ cue up: Halloween XIII
2025

New collaborations

Singles with 33 Below, Deadcrow and WINK, three artists from the label's newer wave.

▶ cue up: ALLNIGHTER (with WINK)

What he means to us

John, here is the short version: RL Grime is the anchor. I have seen him live at least 28 times that I can count, seven in 2019 alone: from a Vegas club to a forest in Canada, Halloween at the Palladium and the Forum, a Triple Crown up and down California, and NiteHarts in San Diego.

What I love is the build. He makes a whole room hold its breath and then lets it go together. That is what these nights have been for me and my people: Brennan beside me in Brooklyn and Austin, Mason in Hollywood, strangers pulling me to the center of the floor.

You are a gamer, so you already know the feeling: the boss fight you have been building toward, and the moment it lands. That is an RL Grime drop. I can't wait to share one with you.

LYMI, Gordon
